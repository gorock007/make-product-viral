# Scoring Rubric

## Per-principle score (0–3 or N/A)

Score each of the 32 principles independently:

| Score | Meaning | Emoji |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 3 | Fully embodies the principle — exemplary | 🟢 |
| 2 | Mostly there — good, with minor gaps | 🟡 |
| 1 | Weakly present — attempted but falls short | 🟠 |
| 0 | Absent or actively violated | 🔴 |
| N/A | Genuinely doesn't apply (justify it) | ⚪ |

Anchor every score to evidence you actually saw. If you can't quote the headline, CTA, price, or section that justifies the score, you haven't looked hard enough.

### Using N/A honestly
N/A is for principles that don't apply to this *type* of product, not principles the product simply fails. Examples:
- #1 / #8 / #27 (no free plan / hard paywall / no subscription) → N/A for a deliberately free open-source or community tool, OR for a product where recurring billing is intrinsic (hosting, infrastructure). Note the reasoning.
- #15 (visible founder) is rarely N/A — most products *can* show a founder.
- When in doubt, score it (usually 0 or 1), don't N/A it. Overusing N/A inflates the result.

## Overall score (out of 100)

```
applicable = principles not marked N/A
overall = round( sum(scores over applicable) / (3 × count(applicable)) × 100 )
```

Example: 30 applicable principles, total 54 points → 54 / (3×30) × 100 = **60/100**.

## Tier labels

| Score | Tier | One-line read |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 0–39 | 🔴 Not viral yet | The public surface doesn't yet sell or spread. Big wins available. |
| 40–59 | 🟠 Getting warm | Foundations exist; hero, proof, and pricing need real work. |
| 60–79 | 🟡 Close | Solid. A handful of sharp fixes could push it over the line. |
| 80–100 | 🟢 Viral-ready | Nails the public surface. Polish the remaining gaps. |

## Grading discipline

- **Be tough.** Most real early-stage products land 40–65. A 90 means the page could be a case study. Don't hand out 3s for "fine."
- **Reward execution, not intent.** A missing testimonials section is 0, not 2-for-trying.
- **Weight your verdict toward the high-leverage principles** even though the math is flat: #20 (sellable from hero), #7/#17/#18 (headline), #10/#25 (show/try), #22/#28 (CTA), #29 (testimonials), #16/#12 (pricing). A product that fails these feels un-viral even if it scores points on minor ones — say so in the verdict.
- **Every 0 and 1 needs a fix** in the Top Fixes section, written concretely.
